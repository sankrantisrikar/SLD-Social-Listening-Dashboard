TROUBLESHOOTING:

❌ Can't find "Application" tab?
   → Try "Storage" tab (Firefox)
   → Try pressing F12 again to refresh

❌ Can't find "li_at" cookie?
   → Make sure you're logged into LinkedIn
   → Refresh the LinkedIn page
   → Check you're on https://www.linkedin.com (not a subpage)

❌ Cookie value is empty?
   → You're not logged in - log into LinkedIn first
   → Try logging out and back in

❌ "Invalid cookie" error?
   → Cookie expired - get a fresh one
   → Make sure you copied the entire value
   → No spaces at start/end

SECURITY WARNING:

🔒 Your li_at cookie is SENSITIVE!

   • It's like your LinkedIn password
   • Anyone with it can access your account
   • Don't share it with anyone
   • Don't post it online
   • Don't commit it to Git

   Keep it private and secure!
