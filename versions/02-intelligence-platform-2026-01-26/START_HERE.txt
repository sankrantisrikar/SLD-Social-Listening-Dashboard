🎯 WHAT IS THIS?

A unified platform for monitoring social conversations about U.S. interventional
pain management across LinkedIn, Twitter, Reddit, YouTube, and policy news.

Features:
• Multi-platform selection (toggle ON/OFF)
• Unified dashboard with CEO summary
• AI chat assistant (platform-scoped responses)
• Spike detection & trend analysis
• Sentiment tracking
• Influencer identification
